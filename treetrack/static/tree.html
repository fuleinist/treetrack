How the dose is calculated

Demand is ET0 over seven days, in millimetres, times the bed area in square metres. One millimetre over one square metre is one litre.

The demand is then scaled by a species factor, an age factor and a mulch factor. Only rain above 2 mm counts as a credit, at 80 percent capture. Logged waterings count in full.

The result rounds up to whole buckets. Established trees need little or no water. The app shows the inputs, so you can correct them.
